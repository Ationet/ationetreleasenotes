# RevoPOS - v2.1.21

## Release Date:
- Verifone T650p: Nov 04, 2026
- Newland N910: Nov 04, 2026
- Urovo i9000s: Nov 04, 2026

### Improvements to Points Accumulation and Redemption Receipts

The following information and display changes have been added to points accumulation and redemption receipts:

- Accumulation:
  - Renamed "Points" to "Points Added".
  - Renamed "Balance" to "Available Points".
- Points Redemption:
  - Added the customer name.
  - Renamed "Points" to "Points Redeemed".
  - Renamed "Balance" to "Available Points".

### Other Enhancements and Changes

- Renamed "Postpaid" to "Sale" in Stand Alone mode.

### Fixed Issues

- Fixed an inconsistency in the sale summary and receipt when the "Use Company Price" option was enabled.
- Fixed an Internal Server Error that occurred when scanning a new national ID card.
- Fixed an issue where users could not return to the previous screen during an odometer request.

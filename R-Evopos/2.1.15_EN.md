## New Features

### Use Company Price for Stand Alone Mode

A new parameter called "Use Company Price" is now available under **Controller → Stand Alone**.

When this parameter is enabled, the terminal will use the prices configured in Contracts or Distribution Prices to calculate the quantity or amount to be dispensed, instead of the price configured on the terminal.

The Company Price will also be used for receipts and batch closing.

### Other Features or Enhancements
* SKU transactions can now use an amount instead of a quantity.
